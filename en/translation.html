Translation and language editing for education research

Abstracts, articles, thesis chapters and questionnaires, by a teacher who also does research in education. Everything is done in writing.

What I do

I translate and edit education research texts. Abstracts, articles, thesis chapters, questionnaires and task instructions written for pupils or teachers.

Languages

Translation
Into Spanish, my mother tongue, and into French, the language I work in every day in Vaud schools. The source text can be in French, Spanish or English.
Language editing
Texts written in French, Spanish or English. For English, I hold the Cambridge C2 Proficiency certificate.

What difference it makes

I know the vocabulary of the field because I use it. I teach in the schools of the canton of Vaud, I am doing a master’s in education research (UNIR), and I submitted an article to a peer-reviewed journal in November 2026.

A term like “formative assessment” or “task instruction” does not have the same equivalent in every school system. I translate for the reader in the country the text is going to.

How it works

Contact
By email, from the first message to delivery.
Quote
You send the text, the target language and your deadline. I reply with a price and a delivery date.
Delivery
The translated or edited text, with my questions on any ambiguous passages.
Price

About

Pablo Correa Prieto is a primary teacher recognised by the CDIP (years 3H–8H) and teaches in the schools of the canton of Vaud. Alongside this he is doing a master’s in education research (UNIR). He speaks French, Spanish (mother tongue) and English.
