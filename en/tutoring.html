Private tutoring · Lausanne and online

Private tutoring with a teacher from Vaud schools

French, maths, English, Spanish and support for pupils on the autism spectrum. At your home, at mine or online. The first hour is free.

Why a teacher

I am a primary teacher recognised by the CDIP and I teach in schools in the canton of Vaud, from primary to lower secondary. I know the Plan d’études romand, the curriculum used in class, and how pupils are assessed. So lessons focus on what is actually expected, at the pupil’s pace.

Subjects

French
Reading, comprehension and writing, from 3P to 11e (Vaud school years).
Maths
Filling gaps in the basics, understanding a concept rather than memorising it, preparing for a test, up to 11e.
English
Conversation, grammar and test preparation. I have taught English to bridging classes (12RAC) in the canton of Vaud. Cambridge C2 Proficiency and TEFL (300 hours) certified.
Spanish
Spanish is my mother tongue. Lessons for beginners and for children who speak Spanish at home and want to read and write it well.
Support for pupils with autism
I have supported a child on the autism spectrum one to one, working with the educational team, and I am trained in this kind of support. Sessions are calm and predictable, with a clear structure and a pace that suits the pupil.

How it works

First hour
Free. We look at what is getting in the way and set a goal.
Rate
CHF 50 per hour, CHF 75 for 90 minutes.
Times
Weekdays 5 to 8 pm, Wednesdays 2 to 8 pm, Saturdays 9 am to 1 pm.
Where
At the pupil’s home in the Lausanne area, at mine near Lausanne, or online.
Language
French, English or Spanish.

As a rule, I do not tutor pupils from my own classes.

Book the first hour

Write to me with the subject, the pupil’s school year and the times that suit you. I reply by email.

My email address is on the home page.

About

Pablo Correa Prieto is a primary teacher recognised by the CDIP (years 3H–8H) and teaches in the schools of the canton of Vaud. Alongside this he is doing a master’s in education research (UNIR). He speaks French, Spanish (mother tongue) and English.
